Usage:
  queue ai providers [--json]
  queue ai models [--provider NAME] [--json]
  queue ai health [--json]
  queue ai health --provider NAME --model NAME --set-state STATE [--reason TEXT] [--cooldown-seconds N] [--json]
  queue ai health --provider NAME --model NAME --clear [--json]
  queue ai health --prune-expired [--json]
  queue ai health --clear-all [--json]
  queue ai health --events [--limit N] [--provider NAME] [--model NAME] [--action ACTION] [--state STATE] [--since-seconds N] [--summary] [--json]
  queue ai health --prune-events [--max-events N] [--max-age-seconds N] [--json]
  queue ai explain [--profile NAME] [--capability csv] [--json]
  queue ai chat [--profile NAME] [--capability csv] [--message TEXT|--input-file FILE] [--live] [--json]
  queue ai json [--profile NAME] [--schema FILE] [--message TEXT|--input-file FILE] [--live] [--json]

Purpose:
  Resolve provider/model choices from AI profiles, provider registry, health,
  capability, cost, and locality constraints. Explain and response JSON include
  bounded health_summary evidence for candidate/rejection review. By default it performs selection
  only; --live delegates to an existing ask-provider helper only when
  QUEUEBASH_AI_LIVE_ENABLED=1 is set by policy.

Policy inputs:
  /etc/bashqueues/policies.d/ai-profiles/*.env
  /etc/bashqueues/policies.d/ai-broker/provider-registry.json
  /etc/bashqueues/policies.d/ai-broker/health-cache.json
  $QUEUEBASH_ROOT/ai-broker/health-cache.json
  $QUEUEBASH_ROOT/ai-broker/health-events.jsonl

Important:
  queue ai broker output is advisory data. It is never evaluated as shell.

AI broker profile health gates:
  AI_ALLOWED_HEALTH_STATES and AI_BLOCKED_HEALTH_STATES constrain selectable provider/model health states. disabled_by_policy and disabled_by_cost remain hard skips. Active policy is reported in health_summary.health_policy.
