Usage: queue vcs detect [PATH] [--json]
       queue vcs probe [PATH] [--json] [--type TYPE] [--timeout SECONDS]
       queue vcs types [--json]

Read-only VCS tenant diagnostics for Git, Subversion, CVS, Mercurial, and
Perforce. Existing git:* assets remain first-class; queue vcs is a neutral
operator surface for mixed and legacy estates. Probe is also read-only and reports
normalised identity/revision/clean-tree facts for audit gates.
